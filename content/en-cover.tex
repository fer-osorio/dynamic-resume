% Opening paragraph body — per-role variants
% =============================================================================
\newcommand{\openingBodyCrypto}{%
    \ifx\OpeningHook\empty
        As a cryptography specialist with deep expertise in post-quantum systems and
        production-grade implementations, I was immediately drawn to \textbf{\CompanyName}.%
    \else
        \OpeningHook%
    \fi{}
    \ifx\FocusArea\empty\else
        \ Your work in \textbf{\FocusArea} aligns directly with my research and
        engineering background.%
    \fi{}
    \ I have spent the last two years designing and optimizing NIST-compliant
    cryptographic protocols---including NTRU-based post-quantum key encapsulation
    and AES encryption libraries---achieving measurable performance improvements
    while maintaining rigorous security properties.
    \ifx\PositionTitle\empty\else
        \ The \textbf{\PositionTitle} role represents exactly the kind of challenge
        I am looking to take on next.%
    \fi%
}

\newcommand{\openingBodySecurity}{%
    \ifx\OpeningHook\empty
        As a security engineer specializing in cryptographic implementations, I was drawn
        to \textbf{\CompanyName} and your commitment to building resilient, standards-compliant
        security infrastructure.%
    \else
        \OpeningHook%
    \fi{}
    \ifx\FocusArea\empty\else
        \ Your focus on \textbf{\FocusArea} maps directly to my hands-on experience.%
    \fi{}
    \ I have built production-grade cryptographic systems from the ground up---implementing
    NIST-validated AES libraries, post-quantum key exchange protocols, and comprehensive
    security testing frameworks---always with an emphasis on correctness, compliance,
    and measurable security guarantees.
    \ifx\PositionTitle\empty\else
        \ I believe the \textbf{\PositionTitle} opening is a strong mutual fit.%
    \fi%
}

\newcommand{\openingBodySoftware}{%
    \ifx\OpeningHook\empty
        I am a software engineer with a security and cryptography specialization,
        and \textbf{\CompanyName}'s engineering culture caught my attention immediately.%
    \else
        \OpeningHook%
    \fi{}
    \ifx\FocusArea\empty\else
        \ Your emphasis on \textbf{\FocusArea} resonates with the kind of problems
        I most enjoy solving.%
    \fi{}
    \ My work centers on high-performance C/C++ systems with robust cryptographic
    foundations---clean architecture, comprehensive testing, and code that lasts.
    I have delivered a 20$\times$ throughput improvement in a post-quantum cryptosystem
    and a $2\times$ performance gain in an AES library, both through careful algorithmic
    analysis rather than premature optimization.
    \ifx\PositionTitle\empty\else
        \ I am excited about the \textbf{\PositionTitle} position and the technical
        challenges it entails.%
    \fi%
}

\newcommand{\openingBodyApplied}{%
    \ifx\OpeningHook\empty
        As an applied cryptographer bridging theoretical research and production-quality
        software, I was compelled to reach out to \textbf{\CompanyName}.%
    \else
        \OpeningHook%
    \fi{}
    \ifx\FocusArea\empty\else
        \ Your work in \textbf{\FocusArea} sits precisely at the intersection of
        the theory and practice I care most about.%
    \fi{}
    \ My Master's research on post-quantum secure communication protocols produced
    both peer-reviewed contributions and working C++ implementations; I see no value
    in theory that cannot be deployed, nor in code that lacks formal foundations.
    \ifx\PositionTitle\empty\else
        \ The \textbf{\PositionTitle} role looks like an ideal home for that philosophy.%
    \fi%
}

\newcommand{\openingBodyGeneral}{%
    \ifx\OpeningHook\empty
        I am a computer scientist and mathematician who likes to solve complex problems
        elegantly---so, to the surprise of no one, when I came across \textbf{\CompanyName}
        I was compelled to reach out.%
    \else
        \OpeningHook%
    \fi{}
    \ifx\FocusArea\empty\else
        \ Your work in \textbf{\FocusArea} is exactly the kind of challenge
        I am looking for.%
    \fi{}
    \ My background spans post-quantum cryptography, symmetric encryption, statistical
    security analysis, and web development---I am most energized when I can bring
    mathematical rigor to engineering problems that matter.%
}

\newcommand{\closingBodyEngineering}{%
    I am reaching out because my work philosophy resonates with what great engineering
    teams stand for: rigorous thinking, clean execution, and a genuine commitment to
    making things that work well and last. I am confident I can contribute meaningfully
    to \textbf{\CompanyName}'s goals from day one.%
}

\newcommand{\closingBodyApplied}{%
    I am reaching out because I believe the best applied cryptography happens when
    theorists and engineers work in the same room---or are the same person. I would
    welcome the opportunity to bring that perspective to \textbf{\CompanyName}'s team.%
}

\newcommand{\closingBodyGeneral}{%
    I am reaching out because my work philosophy and my ethics resonate with what
    \textbf{\CompanyName} stands for. I work hard to see problems as they are,
    and harder still to solve them elegantly---and I know I do my best work alongside
    people who hold themselves to the same standard.%
}
